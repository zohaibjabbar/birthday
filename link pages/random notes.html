02

What You Mean to Me

And from this distance, I want to send you every ounce of love I have stored in me, all wrapped up in words because my arms can't reach you just yet.

03

A Love That Travels Across Miles

Long-distance love is not easy — anyone who says otherwise has never truly lived it. It's the ache of wanting to hold someone and having nothing but a screen to look at. It's falling asleep wishing they were beside you, and waking up to a good morning text instead of their voice. But Nessa, I want you to know that every single day of this distance has only made me more certain about you, more sure of what we have, and more grateful for every moment we do get to share, even through a phone screen. Today, on your birthday, I wish I could teleport across every mile between us and surprise you with open arms. I wish I could see your face light up, hear your laugh, and sit beside you as you blow out your candles. But since I can't, please know that my heart is making that journey anyway. Love doesn't need a flight ticket — mine has already arrived at your doorstep.

05
